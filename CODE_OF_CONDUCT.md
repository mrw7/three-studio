# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Three Studio to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of
experience, identity, background or employer. We pledge to make participation
harassment-free and to treat each other with respect.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct about technical problems and generous with context.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good faith, but ask for evidence when a claim matters.
- Respect privacy: do not publish another person's credentials, private data or
  security report without consent.
- Keep discussions on-topic and move long side conversations elsewhere.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting malicious code, backdoors, credential stealers or
  prompt-injection payloads disguised as contributions.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, chat and any other
project space, and to public interactions where someone is representing the
project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct problems to the maintainer through a private GitHub security
advisory or a direct message. Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. The maintainer may warn, remove content, temporarily restrict
participation or ban a participant, depending on severity and repetition.
Retaliation against a reporter is itself a violation.
